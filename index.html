About Us

Australia-based, globally minded.

CDR Australia, also known as CDRAustralia.Org, is an Australia-based CDR writing company, graded ‘A’ for its excellent support to engineers looking for migration skills assessment and competency assessment for registration or accreditation.

We provide premium technical support services, known as skills assessment report writing, to engineers and ICT professionals for migration, accreditation and registration purposes, with local offices across Australia, Canada, New Zealand and India.

  • Engineers and ICT professionals supported
  • Australian, Canadian, New Zealand, UK and African pathways
  • Offices in four countries, one point of contact

Who we help

Engineers and ICT professionals who need a clear, well-structured competency or skills assessment report for the body assessing them.
